Section 01

1. Overview & Scope

EcoTrack ("we", "us", "our") provides an advanced carbon tracking platform designed to help individuals, organizations, and communities monitor, understand, and reduce their environmental impact.

This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, the rigorous security measures we implement to protect it, our transparent use of artificial intelligence, and your statutory rights to access and delete your records.

Section 02

2. Data Collection & Carbon Metrics

To calculate your precise greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and generate actionable reduction pathways, EcoTrack collects and processes the following categories of data:

Transport & Commute Data

Vehicle types, fuel variants (electric, petrol, diesel, hybrid), daily travel distances, flights, and public transit usage.

Energy & Utility Consumption

Electricity consumption (kWh), natural gas volume, heating source specifications, and green energy supplier tariffs.

Lifestyle & Diet Profiles

Dietary selections (plant-based, vegetarian, omnivore), localized grocery sourcing habits, and household consumption factors.

Waste & Recycling Records

Estimated landfill waste tonnage, composting habits, and material recycling volumes.

Calculation Engine: All emission computations rely on published emission factors from the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) and the Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs (DEFRA).
Section 03

3. Third-Party AI Services (OpenAI API)

EcoTrack incorporates generative artificial intelligence capabilities powered by OpenAI APIs to provide personalized, context-aware sustainability recommendations and carbon-reduction suggestions.

How AI Processes Your Information

  • Sanitized Inputs: Only aggregated, anonymized metrics (e.g. "Monthly transport emissions: 140 kg CO₂e") are transmitted to OpenAI endpoints. Personal identifiers (names, emails, physical addresses) are strictly excluded from AI prompts.
  • Zero Model Training: Under our enterprise API tier agreement, customer prompt data is not used by OpenAI to train, fine-tune, or improve their public foundational models.
  • Actionable Intelligence: AI outputs are strictly constrained to generating carbon-saving habits, meal optimization ideas, and energy reduction tips.
Section 04

4. Security & JWT Authentication

The security of your account and environmental activity records is foundational to our platform architecture:

  • JSON Web Token (JWT) Authentication: Secure, cryptographically signed JWTs manage your authenticated sessions. Tokens are stored securely with strict expiration windows to prevent unauthorized session hijacking.
  • End-to-End Transport Encryption: All client-to-server traffic is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS protocols.
  • Restricted Database Isolation: PostgreSQL persistence utilizes salted hashing (BCrypt) for user credentials and row-level access controls to isolate individual carbon ledgers.
Section 05

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain complete ownership over your data. In accordance with GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), you are entitled to the following rights:

Right to Erasure (Data Deletion)

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated emission logs at any time from your Profile Settings or via support.

Right to Data Portability

You may export your complete carbon footprint history in CSV or PDF format directly through our Reports module.

Right to Rectification

You can edit or remove inaccurate activity entries, recalculating your overall EcoScore and monthly footprint dynamically.

Instant Deletion Guarantee: When you delete your account, all activity logs, goal trackers, and profile metadata are permanently purged from active databases within 24 hours.
Section 06

6. Data Retention & Cookie Policy

We retain your carbon activity records only as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill our analytical services. We use essential session cookies and local storage exclusively to maintain authentication states and theme preferences (e.g. Dark Mode). We do not sell user data to advertising networks.
