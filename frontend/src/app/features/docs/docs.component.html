Core Module

2. How to Log Daily Activities

Navigate to the Carbon Tracker to record emission-generating events across four primary categories:

Transport & Commute

Tracks vehicle travel, public transit, and flight itineraries.

Inputs: Mode (Petrol Car, Diesel, EV, Bus, Train, Flight), Distance (km or miles).
Emission = Distance (km) × Emission_Factor (kg CO₂e/km)

Energy & Utilities

Monitors household or facility electricity and heating fuel usage.

Inputs: Fuel Type (Grid Electricity, Natural Gas, Solar Offset), Quantity (kWh / therms).
Emission = Consumption (kWh) × Grid_Intensity (kg CO₂e/kWh)

Food & Diet

Calculates dietary lifecycle impacts based on consumption patterns.

Inputs: Diet Profile (High Meat, Average Omnivore, Pescatarian, Vegetarian, Vegan).
Emission = Meal_Count × Lifecycle_Dietary_Factor

Waste & Recycling

Accounts for landfill methane generation and diverted recycling credits.

Inputs: Municipal Solid Waste (kg), Organic Composting, Recycled Plastics/Paper.
Emission = Landfill_kg × 0.58 - Recycled_kg × 0.12
Pro Tip: Logging activities on the same day they occur earns a 1.5x Daily Streak XP Multiplier!

5. Standard Emission Factors Reference

EcoTrack utilizes validated reference factors based on UK Government GHG Conversion Factors (DEFRA 2024) and IPCC Fourth Assessment Report (AR4):

Category Activity Mode Standard Emission Factor Units
Transport Petrol Passenger Car 0.170 kg CO₂e per passenger-km
Transport Diesel Passenger Car 0.165 kg CO₂e per passenger-km
Transport Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) 0.045 kg CO₂e per passenger-km (grid avg)
Energy National Grid Electricity 0.207 kg CO₂e per kWh consumed
Energy Natural Gas Heating 0.183 kg CO₂e per kWh consumed
Food High Meat Diet (>100g/day) 7.19 kg CO₂e per day
Food Plant-Based / Vegan Diet 2.89 kg CO₂e per day
